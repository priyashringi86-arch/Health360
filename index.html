Part 1: Introduction & Main Dashboard Overview

"Good morning ma'am and everyone! My name is Priya Shringi, enrollment number EN25CA3020119, and my email is en25ca3020119@medicaps.ac.in. Today, I am presenting my Tableau project titled 'Smart Healthcare Analytics: Patient Health Insights & Risk Factor Analysis'.

The primary objective of this project is to analyze comprehensive patient health data to identify key disease risk factors. This dashboard is built on a dataset of 5,000 patients. As highlighted in our KPI cards, we evaluated 1,746 cases of Diabetes, 1,499 cases of Heart Disease, and 1,243 cases of Stroke. Overall, this interactive dashboard provides detailed insights into critical health metrics like cholesterol levels, blood pressure distribution, physical activity, and BMI categories."

Part 2: Health Care 360 Story Walkthrough (Sheet by Sheet)

  • 1. Alcohol & Smoking: "Moving to our Health Care 360 Story, our first point compares alcohol and smoking consumption trends across different age groups using a grouped bar chart."
  • 2. Avg Blood Pressure: "Next, this line chart illustrates how average blood pressure fluctuates across various age groups, from teenagers to elderly patients."
  • 3. Gender-wise Cholesterol: "Here, we analyze the distribution of cholesterol levels categorized by gender and respective age groups."
  • 4. Patient Distribution: "This pie chart highlights the demographic distribution of our patients across Teenagers, Young Adults, Adults, Middle-aged, and Elderly categories."
  • 5. Exercise Levels: "In this tab, we explore physical activity levels and exercise routines corresponding to each age bracket."
  • 6. Heart Disease Distribution: "Here, we highlight which age groups experience a higher volume of heart disease cases."
  • 7. Heart Disease by Age: "This bar graph plots the average heart disease rates mapped directly against age groups."
  • 8. BMI Category vs Heart Disease: "In this visualization, we examine how different Body Mass Index categories—such as normal, overweight, or obese—correlate with heart disease risk."
  • 9. Avg Cholesterol by Age: "And finally, this bar chart showcases the average cholesterol levels evaluated across all age groups. Together, this dashboard and story provide a comprehensive 360-degree analytical view of healthcare risk factors. Thank you so much for your time and attention!"

Dataset Scope

Analytics Engine

Responsive single-page web architecture for embedding visual dashboards, presentation scripts, and documentation.
